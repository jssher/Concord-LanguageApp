# Picture-dictionary gaps

A missing translation is stored on the card as `[[en:ENGLISH]]` in both `word` and `sentence`.
Search the packs for the literal text `[[en:`.

[pic-gaps.tsv](pic-gaps.tsv) is the index: `lang`, `id`, English word, English sentence.

Do not invent Aurali (`aur`) or Egyptian (`egy`). Those two languages are entirely markers.
A loanword that this language really uses is a finished translation and is not a marker.
